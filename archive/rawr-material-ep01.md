## VHS RETRO POST RECIPE (CapCut)

Apply to the whole timeline so every shot shares one grain:
1. **VHS/grain overlay** — a "VHS damage" overlay clip set to *Screen* blend, ~30–40% opacity. Add horizontal tracking-line jitter.
2. **Chroma split** — slight RGB/chromatic-aberration offset (±2–4px) — heaviest on caption reveals.
3. **OSD overlay** — top corners in a pixel/mono font: `● REC` (blinking dot) + `SP  EP.01` + a fake timecode. This is your watermark.
4. **Slight desaturation + warm crush** — pull saturation ~ -10, lift blacks a touch so it reads "old tape."
5. **Camera shake** — light shake on the sting and the action shot only, not the whole video.
6. **Captions** — Anton, all-caps, white with one word in bite-red (#FF2A1F), pop-in on the beat.
