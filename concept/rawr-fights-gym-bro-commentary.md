# RAWR FIGHTS — GYM-BRO COMMENTARY PLAYBOOK 🦖🏋️

Two commentators calling kaiju dino brawls like it's fight night at the gym. Play-by-play gets hyped; color-commentary drops the (real) stats. The comedy is treating apex predators like guys arguing about their bench max.

---

## THE TWO VOICES
- **THE HYPE MAN (play-by-play):** loud, breathless, all-caps energy. Calls the action. Lives for the KO.
- **THE STAT BRO (color):** calmer "well actually" gym-scientist. Drops the real numbers mid-hype. This is your accuracy moat *in character* — he's the one who says "that bite's 57,000 newtons, bro, that's peer-reviewed."

Together: Hype Man says something insane → Stat Bro backs it with a real fact. That contrast IS the show.

---

## THE GYM-BRO DICTIONARY (use liberally, don't overdose)
- **mogging / mogged** — dominating. "T-Rex is MOGGING him rn."
- **natty / not natty** — natural. "No way that bite's natty."
- **PR / rep max** — personal record. "New bite-force PR!"
- **cooked / done / fried** — losing badly. "Spino is COOKED."
- **W / L / took the L** — win/loss. "Ankylo just handed him an L."
- **built different** — genetically superior. "Armored, clubbed tail — built different."
- **skipped leg day / arm day** — the weakness roast. "Bro's arms? Skipped every arm day for 66 million years."
- **certified / it's over** — hype confirmation.
- **rent free** — living in someone's head.
- **gyatt / that's crazy / nah he's him** — reaction filler.
- **the pump / veins** — flexing. "Look at the pump on that neck."
- **spotter** — help. "No spotter's saving him from THAT."
- **ratio'd** — outdone.

**Golden rule:** the Stat Bro never lies for a joke. Hype can be dumb; the numbers stay real.

## COMMENTARY BEATS (map to the 55s blueprint)
- **Entrances:** Hype Man screams the name; Stat Bro reads the tale of the tape as "gym stats."
- **Bell:** "IT'S GO TIME."
- **Big hit:** Hype Man loses it; Stat Bro explains *why* it worked (real biomechanics).
- **Comeback:** "HE'S NOT DONE — HE'S NOT DONE."
- **Finisher:** Hype Man does the full "OHHHH" KO call; Stat Bro delivers the receipt as the mic-drop.

## VO PRODUCTION TIPS
- **Two takes, your own voice, pitched slightly different** (or use two ElevenLabs voices) — but your own voice is safest for monetization and gives it personality.
- Keep the Hype Man **loud and clipping**; keep the Stat Bro **dry, close-mic'd, chill.** The tonal gap is the joke.
- **Cut commentary to the beat** — Hype lines land on impacts, Stat Bro lines fill the momentum lulls.
- **One real fact per fight, minimum**, delivered by Stat Bro. That's the pin-comment receipt spoken out loud.
- Don't bury the roast — every fight needs one "skipped [X] day" line.
