Two city-sized dinos, WWE-style walkouts, then they level a downtown. Real stats still decide the winner — hype the spectacle, keep the receipts.

**Global look:** every clip is **9:16 · photorealistic · CITY / KAIJU SCALE (dinosaur towering over skyscrapers) · cinematic blockbuster lighting · "no text."** VHS grain + WWE nameplates + stat cards + health bars + `LIVE` bug all go on in CapCut. ⭐ = hero shot (spend Veo 3.1 / Sora 2 credits).

## 🎤 THE WWE ENTRANCE FORMULA (3 seconds, per fighter)

Three micro-beats inside one 3s clip (or two ~1.5s clips cut together):
1. **0.0–1.0s — the reveal:** darkness/smoke between skyscrapers, a spotlight snaps on, silhouette of the beast.
2. **1.0–2.0s — the pop:** it bursts forward / roars, pyro columns fire up on both sides.
3. **2.0–3.0s — the pose:** hero low-angle "power stance" — hold for the nameplate slam.

**Post (CapCut) over the entrance:**
- WWE-style **nameplate lower-third** — name in Anton, home turf ("LATE CRETACEOUS · N. AMERICA"), pyro sparks.
- **TALE OF THE TAPE stat card** wipes in: `HP · ATK · SPD · BITE · WEIGHT` (use the real numbers below).
- **SFX:** entrance-music stab + crowd ROAR + a hype announcer line.
- Chroma-split + camera shake on the pyro hit.

**Announcer bark templates (VO):**
> "Weighing NINE TONS… the tyrant king himself… TYYYRANNOSAURUS REX!"
> "From the rivers of Cretaceous Egypt… the longest predator to EVER walk… SPINOOO-SAURUS!"

## 🎬 PRODUCTION NOTES
- **Sell the scale:** always include a human-scale reference in the city shot (cars, buses, tiny helicopters) so the dinos read as kaiju-huge, not normal-sized.
- **Health bars** run across the top the whole fight and drain on big hits — that's the WWE/game-broadcast glue.
- **Credits:** ~2 entrances + staredown + 4 battle clips + finisher = ~8 clips/fight. Kling for the mid-battle beats, Veo/Sora on the ⭐ entrance + finisher. Budget ~250–350 cr per fully-loaded fight.
- **Audio:** entrance-music stabs → phonk during the brawl → bass-drop on the finisher → crowd roar throughout. Keep it licensed (Uppbeat/Epidemic).
